**Prompt:** Quick — why do we yawn?

---

The honest answer: nobody's fully sure. The leading hypothesis is brain cooling — yawning draws in cool air and increases blood flow to the brain, helping regulate its temperature when you're tired or drowsy. It's also tied to arousal states (transitioning between sleep/wake, boredom/alertness) and, separately, contagious yawning seems linked to social empathy circuits rather than temperature at all.
