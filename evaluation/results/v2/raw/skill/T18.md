The arc reactor concept is essentially a compact fusion-like power core. The core idea that makes it interesting isn't magic — it's energy density. Real power sources face a brutal trade-off: batteries are safe but low-density; nuclear fission is dense but needs heavy shielding and large reactors; fusion is extremely dense but we can't yet sustain/contain it at small scale. A palm-sized reactor producing enough power to run a flight suit and life-support simultaneously would require either:

1. **A new fuel/element** (fictional "exotic element") with unusually high energy-per-mass, or

**A few useful angles**

- *Physics*: Real fusion reactors (like tokamaks) need massive magnetic coils to contain plasma at 100+ million °C. Shrinking that to arc-reactor size is the actual unsolved problem — not the energy release, but the *containment*.
