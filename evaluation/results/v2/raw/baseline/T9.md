# Solid-State Battery EVs: State of Play (October 2026)

**Bottom line: Nothing fully "solid-state" is in mass-market EVs yet.** What's actually on roads today are *semi-solid* (hybrid solid-liquid electrolyte) cells, while true all-solid-state batteries remain in pilot-line/validation stage, with real production targeted for 2027–2030.

## What's Already Shipping (Semi-Solid)
- **NIO** sells a 150 kWh pack using a WeLion semi-solid cell — ~577 miles (930 km) of range.
- Several Chinese automakers (FAW, Dongfeng, GAC) have installed semi-solid cells in test/limited vehicles.
- These deliver real range gains (300–350 Wh/kg) but aren't the "holy grail" all-solid-state chemistry.

## Key Players & Timelines

| Company | Status | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Toyota** | Broke ground on solid-electrolyte pilot plant w/ Idemitsu (Jan 2026); prototype shows 1,200 km range, <10 min charge | Limited-batch EVs **2027–2028** (already slipped from original 2020/2023 targets) |
| **QuantumScape** | Shipping B-sample QSE-5 cells to VW/PowerCo; opened "Eagle Line" pilot facility (San Jose, Feb 2026); powered a Ducati race bike at IAA Mobility | No public EV launch date yet |
| **Samsung SDI** | Pilot line running at Suwon; engineering samples boast 600-mile range, 9-min charge, 500 Wh/kg; supplying BMW eval vehicles | Mass production **H2 2027** |
| **BYD** | Sulfide-based pilot plant (2 GWh) commissioned Feb 2026, but yield rates still below 70% | Limited batches **2027**, mass production **~2030** |
| **Nissan** | Pilot line at Yokohama plant | Production vehicle **2028** |
| **Mercedes/Honda** | Mercedes testing Factorial semi-solid in EQS prototype; Honda developing independently | Late this decade |
| **GAC Motor** | Vehicle integration plans starting 2026 | Mass production **2027–2030** |

## Regulatory Push
China is finalizing a national solid-state EV battery standard — comment period closed Feb 2026, final standard expected **July 2026** — signaling serious state-level push toward commercialization.

## Industry Consensus
- **2026–2027**: Semi-solid batteries continue in limited production (NIO-style); pilot lines scale up (Toyota, Samsung, QuantumScape, BYD).
- **2027–2028**: First small-batch, premium-tier EVs with true all-solid-state packs (Toyota, Samsung SDI).
- **~2030**: Broad/affordable mass-market adoption across mainstream models.

The IEA's 2026 assessment is notably cautious, emphasizing that announced pilot lines and prototype dates aren't the same as proven high-volume manufacturing — yield and cost at scale remain unsolved problems (see BYD's sub-70% yield issue as a cautionary example).

**No fully solid-state battery is available in any production EV sold in the U.S. as of October 2026.**
