**Prompt:** My cat likes to sit in boxes that are too small for her.

---

Ha, that's a classic cat behavior! It's actually pretty well-documented — cats are drawn to small, enclosed spaces because it taps into their instincts:

- **Security**: Tight spaces make them feel protected from predators (even if your living room is predator-free).
- **Warmth**: Curling up in a small space helps them conserve body heat.
- **Stress relief**: Studies have shown confined spaces can actually lower cortisol levels in cats — it's calming for them.
- **Ambush instinct**: Small hidden spots are great for watching the world without being seen.

There's even a saying among cat folks: "if it fits, I sits" — and apparently it doesn't even need to fully fit! Cats will happily fold themselves into boxes, bowls, sinks, or baskets that seem comically undersized.
